// common questions

FAQ

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we've shipped to 60+ countries. Every item ships in custom wooden crating with full insurance. We handle all customs documentation.

What's your authenticity guarantee?

Every item comes with a signed Certificate of Authenticity. If an item is found to be inauthentic within 5 years, we offer a full refund.

Can I visit the archive?

Yes, by appointment. We welcome collectors, researchers, and institutions. Contact us to arrange a visit to our Bristol facility.

Do you do restoration work?

We offer restoration services for items already in our inventory. For externally owned items, we do selective consultation on a case-by-case basis.

Are payment plans available?

For items over £1,000, we offer staged payment arrangements for verified collectors. Contact us to discuss terms.

Do you work with museums?

Yes — we have active relationships with several national computing museums and can arrange institutional pricing and long-term loans.
