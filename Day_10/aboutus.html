Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is placed, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your shipment in real-time on our website or the carrier's portal. Tracking updates are sent automatically.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on most products. Items must be in original condition with packaging intact. Simply contact our customer service team to initiate a return. We'll provide you with a prepaid shipping label.

Are all products genuine?

Yes, all products are 100% genuine and sourced directly from authorized distributors. Each item is quality tested before shipment. We provide authenticity certification for high-value products.

Do you offer international shipping?

We currently ship to select countries in Asia and Europe. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. Please check the shipping details at checkout to see if we deliver to your area.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All payments are encrypted and secure using SSL technology.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your personal and payment information. Your data is never shared with third parties. Please review our Privacy Policy for more details.

How long does delivery usually take?

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days, while express delivery is available for 2-3 business days. Delivery times may vary based on location and product availability. Free shipping is available on orders above $50.

What warranty do products come with?

Most products come with manufacturer warranty ranging from 1-2 years. Extended warranty options are available for selected items. You can register your product on our website to activate the warranty.
